My work is grounded in three complementary pillars of scientific psychology.

The **humanistic** approach — inspired by Carl Rogers — places the relationship at the heart of the work. Unconditional positive regard, respect for your own pace, and the belief that everyone is capable of growth are the cornerstones of my practice.

**Cognitive Behavioural Therapy** (CBT) provides concrete, scientifically validated tools to understand and reshape the thoughts, emotions, and behaviours that get in the way of your wellbeing.

**Positive psychology** focuses less on problems and more on resources — strengths, resilience, and meaning. It directs our work toward what is already working and what can be nurtured, rather than what is lacking.

These three approaches are not separate — they work in synergy. I tailor my tools to your unique situation and needs, drawing on one, the other, or a combination of all three.
