---
title: "Why ENSŌ?"
weight: 3
---

In Zen philosophy, the ENSŌ circle is born from a single gesture—a single breath. It is never entirely closed, and that is precisely where its strength lies. Rather than striving for perfection, it reveals the richness of the present moment and the grace of imperfection.

This same ethos guides my practice. Each person I accompany is unique, with their own pace, their own resources, and their own way of moving forward. My role is not to map out the path or define the speed—but to create the space for you to discover it for yourself.
