We all long to thrive.

You're looking to move forward. Not necessarily because something is wrong, but because you want to make the most of your life and your inner strengths.

I offer a supportive space and practical tools to help you gain clarity, discover or strengthen your personal resources, and find the words for the changes you want to make.

For your child. For you.

Your child or teenager may be going through a difficult time or trying to find their way, and you're looking for someone you can trust to support them. Or perhaps you're simply looking for guidance to help you support them more confidently yourself.

I work with children, teenagers, and parents, offering the approach that best meets each person's needs.

Understanding yourself.

You've always experienced the world a little differently. You'd like to understand yourself better and to find a way of living that feels more true to who you are and allows you to feel freer.

Whether or not you have a diagnosis, I offer support tailored to the way you experience the world, helping you move towards a life that reflects what matters most to you.

The support offered falls under psychological counselling and guidance, rather than psychotherapy.

Sessions are not covered by basic health insurance (LAMal).

Some supplementary insurance plans offer partial coverage. Please check with your insurer before booking to confirm your coverage.
